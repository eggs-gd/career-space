## What it is

A productized-services marketplace: sellers publish fixed-scope "Gigs" that buyers purchase off
the shelf, often without a conversation first. Audience: buyers deciding whether a specific
packaged service solves a specific problem at this price and timeline.

## Profile structure

Two distinct levels:

- **Seller profile** — one umbrella identity: Seller Tagline (short "I will …" line), Seller
  Description, Skills, plus a Work Experience section for credibility.
- **Gigs** — separate published products under that seller. Each: title (short, "I will …"
  form) · category · search tags · description · pricing tiers/packages · scope and delivery
  time · FAQ · gallery.

One seller, several Gigs.

## Discovery mechanics

Buyers search and filter by Gig category, tags, price, and delivery time, then buy directly,
usually with no pre-sale conversation. The seller profile is read as a trust signal; the Gig is
the unit of purchase.
