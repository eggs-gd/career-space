# Upwork

Factual description of the platform. How this candidate should use Upwork lives in
`data/surfaces/upwork/context.md`; generic methodology is in `policies/surfaces-framework.md`.

Character limits change; verify against the live platform before relying on an exact number. The
Employment History cap below is a hard platform limit confirmed in use.

## What it is

A client-driven freelance marketplace: buyers post jobs, freelancers submit proposals, the buyer
picks one. The profile is what a buyer reads after a proposal catches their eye. Audience: buyers
evaluating whether this person can solve the problem they're paying for.

## Profile structure

- **Title** — one line under the name, searchable, shown in buyer search results.
- **Overview** — free text. First ~2 lines show before a fold.
- **Employment History** — repeated entries: title · company · dates · description.
  **Description is capped at 1,000 characters** (everything after the date line, including
  achievements and any Technologies line). A role that runs over needs real compression.
- **Other Experience** — title + description only, no date/company fields. Only the first three
  titles show above the fold.
- **Skills** — a tag list; some ("Fractional CTO") are real service categories buyers filter by.
- Also: hourly rate, Specialized Profiles, Portfolio, Certifications, Languages, Education.

## Discovery mechanics

Buyers search by title and skills, and Upwork suggests freelancers for posted jobs based on
title, skills, and history. A buyer typically reads the profile after a proposal, in the context
of the specific job they posted.
