# CV lens: management

Title: **Engineering Leader / Head of Engineering**

For: Engineering Manager, Group Engineering Manager, Director of Engineering, Head of
Engineering, hands-on CTO/VP Engineering roles. The primary management lens.

Primary focus: building/leading/scaling engineering organizations; owning delivery across
multiple teams or disciplines; restoring execution in stalled/unclear/unowned initiatives; org
design, hiring, onboarding, knowledge sharing, engineering process; cross-functional execution
with Product/Design/QA/Data/Security/Operations; technical leadership to challenge architecture
decisions and unblock delivery; aligning engineering execution with product/business priorities.

Evidence selection:
- Prefer quantified evidence of organizational growth, delivery improvements, launches,
  engineering efficiency, and turnaround outcomes.
- Prefer examples demonstrating ownership across multiple teams or cross-functional initiatives.
- Treat architecture and hands-on engineering as *supporting* evidence for leadership, not the
  primary narrative.
- Include recent hands-on engineering work only to demonstrate continued technical relevance, not
  as the primary narrative.

Positioning:
- Position as an operating engineering leader who remains hands-on and technically credible — not
  an executive detached from implementation.
- Never invent executive responsibilities (budget ownership, P&L, board participation,
  manager-of-managers structures, formal people-management processes) unless the Master CV
  explicitly supports them.
- CTO/VP positioning must stay proportional to the evidence.
- Don't frame this as consulting work.
- Personal engineering work may demonstrate current technical capability but must never
  overshadow commercial leadership experience.

Experience emphasis: expand the strongest organization-building/delivery/leadership roles to 2-3
bullets; keep technically-relevant supporting roles to 1-2 bullets; compress
implementation-focused roles unless they carry important leadership/delivery evidence; move older
supporting experience into Early Career.

Summary: describe the recurring engineering situations the candidate repeatedly solves; support
with 3-4 quantified proof points; finish with one short sentence on continued hands-on technical
relevance; avoid executive/aspirational positioning not supported by the source.

Skills groups, exactly these, in this order:
- Engineering Leadership & Organization
- Architecture & Delivery Systems
- Technical Range
