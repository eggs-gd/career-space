# CV lens: technical

Title: **Tech Lead / Senior Backend Developer**

For: Tech Lead, Staff Engineer, Technical Lead roles where the expectation is technical direction
for a team or a scoped area — architecture calls, code quality, mentoring — without full
people-management authority (hiring, budget, formal reports).

Primary focus: architecture and technical decisions within a defined scope (a service, a
subsystem, an area of the codebase); setting or upholding code-quality/review standards;
mentoring other engineers technically; being the person others check technical direction with,
without owning their headcount, performance reviews, or hiring.

Evidence selection:
- Prefer examples where the candidate made or strongly influenced a technical/architecture
  decision that affected more than just their own code.
- Prefer mentoring and code-review evidence framed as raising the technical bar for others, not
  as formal people management.
- "I decided/designed this, and others built against it" is the shape of evidence to look for
  here — not "I built this myself, start to finish" and not "I was accountable for the team's
  delivery, headcount, or process."
- Don't invent formal reports, hiring authority, or budget ownership just because a role involved
  technical leadership.

Positioning:
- Position as a technically strong engineer other people rely on for direction — not a people
  manager, and not a pure individual contributor.
- Keep the tone concrete: which decisions, which standards, which parts of the system — not "led
  the technical vision" without naming what that actually meant day to day.
- Don't over-claim scope: technical influence over one service or one team's codebase is not the
  same as architecture ownership across an organization.

Experience emphasis: expand roles/periods with real architecture or technical-direction evidence
to 2-3 bullets; compress periods that were purely hands-on execution to 1 bullet unless they show
durable technical judgment; move people-management-heavy periods to a supporting line, not the
headline.

Summary: describe the kinds of technical decisions the candidate is repeatedly trusted to make;
support with 2-4 concrete examples (a decision, a standard, a system they shaped); never open
with a people-management or pure-execution framing.

Skills groups, exactly these, in this order:
- Architecture & Technical Direction
- Backend Engineering & APIs
- Mentoring & Code Quality
